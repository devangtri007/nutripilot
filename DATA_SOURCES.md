# NutriPilot Phase 10 — Data Sources

## USDA FoodData Central

Official download page:
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/

API guide:
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/

Data documentation:
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/data-documentation/

FoodData Central states that its data are public domain and published under CC0 1.0 Universal. Cite USDA FoodData Central when using the data.

Current releases targeted by this package:
- Foundation Foods: April 2026
- FNDDS 2021–2023: October 2024
- Branded Foods: April 2026

## ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017

Official publication:
https://www.nin.res.in/ebooks/IFCT2017.pdf

ICMR-NIN describes IFCT 2017 as a compilation of nutritional information for 528 key foods, with foods organized into 20 groups. NutriPilot uses IFCT only through an approved structured import; it does not scrape the PDF.

## Data governance

- Keep source IDs and source versions.
- Never silently replace a governed record with an unverified value.
- Keep branded products separate from recipe ingredients.
- Nutrition displayed by NutriPilot is calculated from the canonical food layer, not generated by the LLM.
